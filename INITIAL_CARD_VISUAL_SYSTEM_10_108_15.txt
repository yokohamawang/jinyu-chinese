Koepanda 10.108.15 — Initial Card Visual System

- Based on 10.108.14; full package preserved.
- b/p/m/f cards use one fixed vertical hierarchy: letter → IPA → articulation/airflow cue.
- b [p]: short/weak airflow cue.
- p [pʰ]: longer/strong airflow cue; superscript h remains IPA aspiration marker.
- m [m]: closed-lip/nasal resonance cue.
- f [f]: narrow continuous airflow cue.
- All four cards are forced into the same line structure; f no longer appears on one line.
- IPA is visually secondary so beginners focus first on the sound and mouth/airflow behavior.
- A subtle bridge to the app's tone identity is added: after learning the initial, connect it to finals + four tones.
- Existing 10.108.14 audio behavior is retained unchanged.
