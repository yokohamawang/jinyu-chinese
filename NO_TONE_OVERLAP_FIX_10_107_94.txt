DEMO 10.107.94

Fixed root cause of intermittent 4th-tone playback being cut off by 3rd tone.

Cause:
- After the fourth tone card was tapped, markAProgress() detected all four tones as heard.
- It scheduled the ear-training target (tone 3) after 180 ms.
- Tone 4 was still playing, so playLocalPinyin(tone 3) stopped tone 4 and replaced it.

Fix:
- Removed the automatic 180 ms ear-training playback completely.
- Ear-training stage is revealed silently.
- Tone 3 plays only when the learner explicitly presses the listen button.
- New JS filename + v=10.107.94 avoids stale Safari/GitHub Pages cache.
