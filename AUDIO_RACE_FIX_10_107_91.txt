Koepanda DEMO 10.107.91 — first-play audio race fix
Base: 10.107.90 FULL_LessonLayerFix

Problem fixed:
- On the first pass through a1 -> a2 -> a3 -> a4, a delayed loadedmetadata event from an older Audio object could start after a newer button press.
- This could make the fourth press audibly replay an earlier tone even though the button mapping was correct.

Fix:
- Added monotonic audioRequestId cancellation token.
- Old Audio elements are paused, detached from src, and invalidated before a new tone starts.
- loadedmetadata/play promise callbacks verify they still belong to the latest request before playback/resolution.
- No pronunciation source, UI, lesson order, tone mapping, or artwork changed.

Long-term production recommendation:
- Bundle one professionally recorded local WAV per syllable/tone. That removes network timing/caching dependence and locks pronunciation quality.
